# Week 13 Reflection

Reflect 13: Do we imagine AI futures as utopian, dystopian, or something in between? What do those visions reveal about us?

I tend to imagine AI futures as in between, and I think that's harder to sit with than either extreme. Pure dystopia lets you off the hook; pure utopia does the same in reverse. What unsettles me more is a future where the headline numbers are good and the cost is buried in a terms of service clause nobody read. Working on this project clarified something: the futures we imagine reveal what we have already accepted. The believable 2047 I built was just 2025, extended forward until it became impossible to ignore.
