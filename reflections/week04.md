# Week 4 Reflection

Reflect 4: Is AI a collaborator, tool, or plagiarist in storytelling?

I think AI can be a collaborator and a tool, but calling it a plagiarist depends on how it is used. As a tool, AI helps generate ideas, outlines, or drafts quickly. It can suggest new directions and make the writing process faster. As a collaborator, it feels like a partner you can brainstorm with, especially when you refine prompts and build on what it gives you. However, AI does not have original experiences or emotions. It creates stories based on patterns it learned from existing texts. Because of that, there is a risk of repeating or closely echoing other works. I do not see AI itself as intentionally plagiarizing, since it does not have intent. The responsibility falls on the human using it. In storytelling, AI works best as support, while the human remains the true author and decision-maker.
