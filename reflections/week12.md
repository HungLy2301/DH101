# Week 12 Reflection

Reflect 12: Is AI sustainable? What ecological trade-offs are we willing to accept?

In its current form, AI is not sustainable. It requires large amounts of water, energy, land, and minerals, and these demands are growing quickly. Data centers are being built in areas that already face water shortages, and the environmental cost is often placed on communities that do not benefit from the technology.
AI could become more sustainable, but this would require better decisions about where and how systems are built, more transparency about resource use, and limits on expansion in sensitive areas. However, current incentives focus more on growth and competition than sustainability. The real issue is not only what trade offs we accept, but who gets to decide them. Right now, the people most affected by environmental costs often have the least influence, which raises important concerns about fairness and responsibility.
