# Week 2 Reflection

Reflect 2: Can AI help us define what it means to be human, or does it reduce us to patterns?

I think AI can do both: it highlights patterns in human behavior, language, and creativity, showing common threads across experiences, but it cannot fully grasp our emotions, values, or intentions. By reflecting our habits and choices, AI can help us see aspects of ourselves more clearly. At the same time, relying on patterns risks reducing the complexity of humanity to data points, overlooking individuality, context, and depth. AI can illuminate certain truths about humans, but it cannot replace the lived, subjective experience that defines what it truly means to be human.
