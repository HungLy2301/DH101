# Week 10 Reflection

Reflect 10: How does AI change our relationship to play, rules, and narrative?

Building an interactive AI narrative made me realize AI doesn't just enter games as a character. It quietly rewrites what the rules mean. When an AI evaluates your choices, it isn't judging the way a human would. It's sorting you. That shift changes how narrative feels: stakes become statistical, authenticity becomes a variable. What I found most unsettling wasn't designing the AI antagonist; it was how naturally game logic and AI logic already resemble each other. Both systems reward the right outputs. Play used to feel like a space apart from that pressure. Working through this project made me wonder if it still is.
