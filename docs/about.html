About Me

Hi — I’m Hung.

I study Financial Economics with a Mathematics minor at Denison University. I bring together analytical thinking, data storytelling, and critical design to question the systems that shape how we live with AI.

This portfolio is built around a semester of work in DH101: Critical Making in the Age of AI. It is designed to show not just the makes, but the decisions, the tools, and the ethics behind them.
