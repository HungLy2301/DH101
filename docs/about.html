About Me

Hi — I'm Harry.

I'm a junior at Denison University studying Financial Economics with a Mathematics minor (GPA 3.75/4.0). My professional foundation is in data analysis, financial modeling, and strategy consulting—but I came into DH 101 to ask a deeper question: what systems are hidden behind the tools we use every day, especially AI?

This semester brought together two sides of how I think. On one side: Excel models, ROI calculations, comparable analysis. On the other: critical mapping, network visualization, speculative design. This portfolio shows that work, and the argument that emerged from it: that AI is not a tool, but a system of power that shapes what gets measured, what gets hidden, and who bears the cost.
