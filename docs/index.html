DH 101 · Spring 2026 · Denison University

AI is not a tool.
It is a system of power.

I came into this semester thinking about AI the way most people do — as a tool. Something you prompt, something that outputs, something useful or occasionally frustrating. I left thinking about it as a system: a set of relationships between labor, infrastructure, capital, and meaning that is designed, at nearly every level, to make itself hard to see.

This portfolio is the record of that shift. Twelve projects — a comic, a GIF, a Twine game, a counter-map, a network diagram, an infographic, a data visualization, a speculative newspaper from 2047 — each one cracking the same assumption from a different angle. The site itself is a fifth artifact: an attempt to make the argument visible.
