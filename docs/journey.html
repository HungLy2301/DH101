/ The Journey

Twelve weeks.
One argument, told twelve ways.

Each project below began as a weekly assignment and ended as a piece of an argument I didn't know I was making until the last few weeks. They are presented in the order I made them so the evolution is visible. Click any card to see the artifact, my reflection on it, and the full attribution of how it was made.

When you reach the end, the final reflection ties them together.
