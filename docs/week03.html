Make 03 · Week 03 Comic · AI image generation Tool: ChatGPT

Cream-Puff Boys

A five-panel fairy comic about sweetness saving the world. What I learned was not about sweetness. It was about formula.

The Artifact

First version, from the original prompt.

Second version, after asking for more emotional resonance.

Your Take

What I asked for

I wanted to explore the idea that sweetness and kindness can create change. I prompted AI to generate a five-panel fairy-style comic about "cream-puff boys" saving the world through sweetness. The panels follow a clear structure: introduction of heroes, appearance of darkness, action through "sugar power," transformation of the villain, and a final moral statement.

What I got

What I got was structurally competent and emotionally familiar. The AI relied on familiar superhero patterns — a dark villain, a dramatic attack, an instant redemption. The panel sequence moves from chaos to harmony in the way every cape-and-cowl story has moved from chaos to harmony for fifty years. AI produces recognizable narrative formulas, not original lived meaning.

Who's actually telling the story

While the AI generated the images and dialogue, the intention came from me. I chose the theme, the moral, and the emotional tone. I acted as the director, deciding what the story should represent. The AI acted as the illustrator and draft writer. The meaning comes from my concept; the execution reflects machine-learned storytelling structures.

The bigger point

AI does not independently "tell" a story. It generates visual and narrative patterns based on training data. Making with AI is collaborative, but human intention still defines purpose and message — and the limit of that collaboration becomes most visible when the machine reaches for formula and the human has to decide whether the formula is the right fit for the meaning they intended.

Attribution & AI Use

Tools used
ChatGPT (image generation based on text prompts)
AI prompts (summary)
Prompt 1: "Create a short comic about cream-puff boys going to save the world with sweetness and 'power-puff-ness'; the moral of the comic is to promote the idea that sweetness can initiate change and save the world. Create a 5-panel comic with cute and fairy drawing style."

Prompt 2: "Make the comic and storyline more emotional, touching, and invoke moral sense to deliver the message of how sweetness can save the world."
What AI generated
Two five-panel comic versions in chibi/fairy art style with dialogue and visual narrative.
What I changed or decided
Story concept, moral theme, prompt design, panel intention. The reflection analyzing AI's tendency toward formula is entirely my own writing. The decision to present both versions (rather than just the "better" one) is deliberate — it makes the formula-default visible.

Weekly reflection · Week 03

What does "authenticity" mean when identity is co-constructed with AI?

When identity is co-constructed with AI, authenticity becomes limited. AI builds identities by combining existing data and visual patterns rather than understanding who a person truly is. Even when humans guide the prompt, the result often reflects surface level traits instead of deeper human qualities. While AI can closely match someone's appearance with enough detail, it struggles to represent internal values, emotions, and lived experiences. Abstract traits like kindness or feeling fulfilled are difficult for AI to visualize. Authenticity in AI-generated identity is more about visual accuracy than truly representing the complexity of a real person.
