## Reflection
In this comic, I wanted to explore the idea that sweetness and kindness can create change in the world. I prompted AI to generate a five-panel fairy-style comic about “cream-puff boys” saving the world through sweetness and “power-puff-ness.” The panels follow a clear structure: introduction of heroes, appearance of darkness, action through “sugar power,” transformation of the villain, and a final moral statement. The panel sequence shapes meaning by visually moving from chaos to harmony, reinforcing the message that kindness can overcome negativity.
While the AI generated the images and dialogue, the intention behind the story came from me. I chose the theme, the moral, and the emotional tone. However, the AI relied on familiar superhero patterns: a dark villain, a dramatic attack, and instant redemption. This shows that AI produces recognizable narrative formulas rather than original lived meaning.
Through this exercise, I realized that AI does not independently “tell” a story. It generates visual and narrative patterns based on training data. I acted as the director, deciding what the story should represent, while the AI acted as the illustrator and draft writer. The meaning of the comic comes from my concept, but the execution reflects machine learned storytelling structures. This project shows that making with AI is collaborative, but human intention still defines purpose and message.
